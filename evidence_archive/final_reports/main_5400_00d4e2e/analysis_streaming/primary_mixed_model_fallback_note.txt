Primary log-p99 mixed-effects model fallback record

The predeclared mixed-effects model was attempted first.

The fitted model was not considered inferentially usable because:
- random-effects covariance was singular;
- estimated group variance was zero;
- log-likelihood was infinite;
- AIC/BIC were negative infinite;
- Hessian was not positive definite;
- random effects could not be predicted from the singular covariance structure.

Accordingly, the predeclared paired Wilcoxon fallback was applied
within each of the 18 fixed experimental conditions using the 60
matched seeds. The resulting 18 p-values were adjusted using the
Holm procedure.

The failed mixed-model coefficients are not used as confirmatory
evidence.
